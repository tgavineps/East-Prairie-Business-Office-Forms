3. Terms and Conditions

  • Full-Year Service Requirement: The full stipend amount is contingent upon the Employee successfully completing the duties and responsibilities of the stipended position for the entire academic year.
  • Early Departure / Incomplete Service: If the Employee is unable to fulfill the duties for the entire academic year (e.g., due to resignation, leave of absence, termination, extensive absences, etc.), the stipend payment will be prorated based on the actual duration of service. The Employee agrees to notify the District's Business Office as soon as possible if they anticipate being unable to complete the full term.
  • Required Form Return: This signed agreement must be returned to the Business Office prior to the first scheduled stipend payment date. Payments will not be issued without a signed agreement on file.
  • Job Responsibilities: The Employee agrees to perform all duties and responsibilities associated with the stipended position as outlined by the District and to adhere to all District policies, procedures, and expectations.
  • District Discretion: The District reserves the right to modify or terminate this Agreement if the Employee fails to meet the obligations of the stipended position, or at the discretion of the District. Any such action will be communicated to the Employee in writing.
  • Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois and the policies of East Prairie School District 73.
