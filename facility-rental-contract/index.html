3. Regulations & Rental Conditions

  • Rental Rate & Duration: Rental fees are billed at $60.00 per hour with a mandatory 2-hour minimum rental block ($120.00 base fee). Rental time begins when facilities are opened and ends when the group departs.
  • District Reservation of Rights & Cancellation: East Prairie School District 73 reserves the right to deny, alter, reschedule, or cancel any rental agreement—either in whole or for specific dates—for any reason, including scheduling conflicts or District operational needs. If the District cancels a rental date due to a facility conflict, any prepaid rental fees for the affected date(s) will be promptly refunded.
  • Scope of Access: Rental access includes only facilities specifically named in this contract, nearby hallways, and restrooms. Classroom space is not available for rental.
  • Prohibited Conduct: Tobacco use, alcoholic beverages, lotteries, and gambling are strictly prohibited on all school property.
  • Fire Safety & Doors: No open flames (candles, lamps) are permitted. Exterior doors must remain locked at all times and must NEVER be propped open. Custodians will let groups in at scheduled times.
  • Custodial Supervision: At least one custodian/supervisor must be on duty during facility use. Additional labor required will be billed to the user.
  • Mandatory Insurance Coverage: Evidence of general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence naming East Prairie School District 73 as an additional insured must be submitted prior to approval.
  • Renter Cancellation Policy: Cancellations by the renting organization must be submitted to the Business Office at least 7 days prior to the scheduled date. Groups will be charged for custodial time if notice is not received within 7 days.

Hold Harmless & Property Damage Agreement: Lessee agrees to all rules and regulations set forth by School District 73. Lessee agrees on behalf of themselves and the renting organization to hold District 73, its Board members, and employees harmless from any liability or bodily injury resulting from facility use. The renting organization assumes full financial liability for any damage occurring to property or equipment during or as a result of the rental.
